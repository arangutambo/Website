04 / STATISTICS

Testing for cryptic species in a coral gene-expression survey

R · PCA · classical MDS · k-means · hierarchical clustering · graduate coursework

Reef coral colonies had been assigned to one species on morphology alone. Given a baseline expression matrix of a few hundred colonies across several hundred loci, did the molecular profiles break into discrete groups, as cryptic species would?

They did not. Both ordinations showed one continuous cloud, cluster support stayed near zero at every k, and the hierarchical solution agreed. The defensible conclusion was a negative one, and the report said so and set out what evidence would settle it. The real work was declining a clustering result that k-means will hand you whether or not the structure is there.
